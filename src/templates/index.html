How to use this

A four-step guide to browsing reason status, running the AI agent, and personalizing priorities.
1

Pick a company free

The sidebar lists every company the tracker knows about — use the filter box to jump to one quickly. Selecting one loads its three investment reasons (revenue growth, operating margin, debt growth) computed straight from SEC XBRL data, no LLM involved. This is the Objective Evidence Layer: deterministic and identical no matter who's looking. The stat chips at the top summarize how many reasons are Supported / Weakened / Broken at a glance; click show evidence on any card to see the exact filed facts (accession number, filing date) behind the number.

2

Ask the AI Agent to investigate ~$0.03–0.08

Ask AI Agent to review hands the same three reasons to a real tool-calling agent. It re-checks each one itself, and for anything uncertain or severe it goes further on its own — pulling a second independent source (the company's own earnings release) or reading the actual 10-Q/10-K narrative to explain why a number moved, not just that it did. The tool calls it actually made are listed above its findings.

3

Create an investor profile free

Click + New profile under "Personalize for an Investor" to describe a specific investor: risk tolerance, investment horizon, how large this holding is as a share of their portfolio, and their objective. Saving it is instant — just stored, no LLM involved yet.

4

Personalize ~$0.02–0.05

Pick a profile and click Personalize. The evidence status feeds into two independent priority judgments for that investor — a free rule-based tier and an LLM-derived tier, shown side by side as Monitor / Watch / Review Soon / Review Now. The evidence status itself is never allowed to change based on who's asking, only how urgently it should matter to them — checked automatically on every call.

↻

Recent activity

Every agent run and personalize call above gets saved. Recent activity in the sidebar shows the full history across every company; the same panel appears scoped to just the current company at the bottom of its page.

Point-in-time only from filings Every status shown is derived only from SEC data that was actually filed as of the analysis date — never from information that wasn't publicly available yet. Live web search (used only by the agent, only for background context) never decides a status, for the same reason.
Not built yet Defining a custom reason beyond the built-in 3 (a reason's metric has to match a real SEC XBRL tag name), and a combined portfolio-level view across holdings.
