## 2. Root causes fixed

| Problem | Cause | Fix (deterministic, general) |
|---|---|---|
| AAPL-02 and CAT-12: values computed by A2's numeric verifier were UNKNOWN | A `derived_metric` from the numeric verifier records only an end date and a `<basis>` in its locator, so v2.1 treated it as having no window | The window is taken from the source XBRL facts the verifier registered in the same ledger. Every component of a ratio must be present and agree on one window, and the duration must match the basis (quarter = 3M, annual = 12M; instant). Otherwise it stays UNKNOWN (`derived_without_verified_provenance`). The status is `COMPATIBLE_AFTER_VERIFIED_DERIVATION`. |
| CAT-07: a forward-looking statement about 2026 was used for a 2025 claim | v2.1 dated every 10-K passage by its document (FY2025) and did not consider the statement's own time | (a) Statement-level years: if the key quote names years and none of them is a claim or comparison year, `MISMATCH QUOTE_REFERS_TO_OTHER_YEAR`. Passages that also name a claim year are still accepted, so not every other-year mention is rejected. (b) Statement time from the key quote: `HISTORICAL_FACT` / `FORWARD_LOOKING` / `MIXED_OR_UNCLEAR`. For a historical claim, FORWARD_LOOKING or MIXED cannot decide. |
| 4 claims ERRORed on 529 | Graph retry was 3 attempts with 2–4 s waits; an ERROR row was never re-run on resume | Client retries with backoff and jitter (2 s × 2ⁿ, cap 60 s, ≤ 5, Retry-After), then a non-transient error; `resume --retry-errors` |

## 6. Token limits

**Evidence on the review call (cap 3,000)**
- Plan A: maximum 2,940.
- v2.1: maximum 2,857 (mean about 1,750).
- 2 of 20 reviews used more than 95% of the cap.
- v2.1.1 asks for longer key quotes (with the period words), so the risk rises.

**Safety**
- Truncation is not silent: max_tokens leads to NoStructuredOutput, then NO_FINAL_VERDICT, then A2's label (tested).
- `stop_reason` and `output_tokens` are logged for every call.
- Reconciliation (cap 1,500): maximum 1,336 in Plan A and 1,277 in v2.1. Low risk.

**Recommendation (not applied)**
- Raise the review cap to 4,096 as a documented experimental change. The review requests are new anyway, so no cache is
  lost.
- Effect on cost: only actual usage is charged. The ledger's worst-case reservation per review call rises by about
  $0.016.
- If the cap is kept at 3,000, expect about 0–10% of claims to end NO_FINAL_VERDICT, and report them separately.
