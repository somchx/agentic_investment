## 2. Period-validation rules (`a3v2-periods-1.0`)

### Claim period

The claim period is resolved in this order:

1. **Balance at a date.** `as of / by / at <date>` (when there is no `quarter|months|year ended`) → instant at that date.
   `balance sheet ended <year>` or `at the end of <year>` → instant at the end of that fiscal year.
2. **Duration ending on a stated date:**
   - `quarter / three months ended <date>` → 3M
   - `six months / first half ended` → 6M
   - `nine months ended` → 9M
   - `(fiscal) year / twelve months ended` → 12M
3. **Named period:**
   - `first…fourth quarter (of) (fiscal) YYYY` and `Qn (fiscal) YYYY`
   - `first/second half of (fiscal) YYYY`
   - `first nine months of (fiscal) YYYY`
   - `in YYYY through the end of <month>`
   - `fiscal (year) YYYY`
   - a bare year `YYYY`
4. **The `reporting_period` field**, used only when the text gives nothing: `FYyyyy`, `FYyyyy Qn`, `yyyy Qn`, `yyyy Hn`.

**Fiscal calendar**
- Built from the company's 10-K and 10-Q filings filed on or before the cutoff.
- Fiscal year and fiscal period come from the XBRL facts; the period end is the report date in SEC's submissions index.
- A window's start = the previous fiscal period end + 1 day.

**Fiscal versus calendar**
- If the claim says "fiscal", or the fiscal year ends within 10 days of 31 December, the fiscal reading is used.
- Otherwise a calendar quarter must coincide with a fiscal quarter end (±10 days), or the period is **UNKNOWN**.
- A calendar year on a non-calendar fiscal year is **UNKNOWN**.

**Not yet reported** (`fourth quarter of fiscal 2026` before the 10-K): the end is projected one year on from the prior
year (`source: projected`). Nothing that has been filed can match it.

**Comparison window:** the same window one year earlier.

### Evidence period

| Evidence | Period |
|---|---|
| XBRL fact | Its own start and end. No start (or start = end) → instant. |
| Period derivation | Its start and end, source `derivation` |
| Other derived_metric without a window | UNKNOWN |
| Filing passage | End = the filing's report date. Duration from period words in the **key quote**, otherwise in the **passage**, otherwise: a 10-K covers its fiscal year (12M); a 10-Q passage without period words is UNKNOWN. For a balance claim, a passage is an instant at its report date. |

**Period words:**
- trailing / last twelve months, TTM, LTM → TTM (12M)
- twelve months ended → 12M
- nine months / first nine months → 9M
- six months / first half / H1 → 6M
- second half / H2 → 6M
- three months / quarter / Qn / quarterly → 3M
- year ended / fiscal YYYY / full-year / annual → 12M

### Compatibility

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| EXACT_MATCH | Same kind; same end (±10 days, the claim's own period = `current`, or one year earlier = `comparison`); the same window start for XBRL, or the same months for a passage |
| COMPATIBLE_AFTER_VERIFIED_DERIVATION | The same as EXACT_MATCH, for a unit made by a derivation |
| MISMATCH | Instant vs duration; a different end; a different duration (`DURATION_DIFFERS:9M_vs_3M`, `TTM_vs_6M`, `TTM_IS_NOT_THE_FISCAL_YEAR`); a window start that differs |
| UNKNOWN | The claim's period is unknown; the evidence period is unknown; or a passage states several durations and the key quote does not name one (`PASSAGE_STATES_SEVERAL_DURATIONS`) |

**A shared end date alone is never enough.** Only EXACT_MATCH and COMPATIBLE_AFTER_VERIFIED_DERIVATION are decisive.

**Derivations** (flow metrics only: Revenue, OperatingIncome, NetIncome, GrossProfit, OperatingCashFlow,
CapitalExpenditure; ratios are not additive)
1. A filed fact with exactly the claim's window. Example: six-month YTD operating cash flow.
2. Otherwise the sum of filed single-quarter facts that covers the window exactly. Every quarter must be present, with
   no gaps.

Each derivation is registered as a `derived_metric` unit with its formula and its source facts (period, value,
accession, form, filed date), for the current and the comparison window.

### Review, reconciliation and guard

| Stage | What v2.1 does |
|---|---|
| Evidence review | Each evidence row carries its own `period_status`, `period_start` and `period_months`. The claim period carries start, end, months and kind. The prompt says that MISMATCH / UNKNOWN cannot be ENTAILS or CONTRADICTS, and that the key quote must name the period. |
| Validation (deterministic) | A finding that declares ENTAILS / CONTRADICTS whose evidence (judged on the key quote) is MISMATCH → `PERIOD_MISMATCH`, and if UNKNOWN → `PERIOD_UNKNOWN`. It is downgraded to RELATED_NOT_DECIDING. |
| Reconciliation | The table carries `period_compatibility` per finding |
| Guard | Citations with a MISMATCH period are removed. A change without a VALID deciding finding is rejected with `INVALID_DECIDING_EVIDENCE:PERIOD_MISMATCH` or `:PERIOD_UNKNOWN`, and A2's label and citations are kept. |
| Labels | No Gold label or annotation is read |

## 8. Remaining limitations

1. **Narrative passages** take their period from period words in the key quote or the passage.
   - When a 10-Q passage covers several durations and the quote does not name one, the result is UNKNOWN. That evidence
     cannot decide.
   - This makes RG stricter on quarterly causal claims. Changes to INSUFFICIENT are accepted more easily than before.
2. **The review output may hit its cap.** In Plan A the review output reached 2,940 of 3,000 tokens. v2.1 adds rows, so
   the risk of truncation, then NO_FINAL_VERDICT, then A2's label, is higher. Not changed (needs approval).
3. **Derivations** cover additive flow metrics only. Ratios, segment figures and amounts that appear only in text are not
   derived.
4. **Projected periods** (ADBE-10, INTC-10) are estimates. They can be used only to show that a period has not been
   reported, never as a match.
5. **Calendar wording on a non-calendar fiscal year** is UNKNOWN by design. dev-v0.2 contains no such claim now, but the
   holdout may.
6. **The period words are an English list.** Unusual phrasing ("year-to-date through …") may not be recognised and gives
   UNKNOWN. This is the safe direction.
7. **The reviewer is still an LLM.** The period checks verify the window, not logical entailment.
8. **The labels are provisional** (AAPL-05; 48 single-labeller claims). Report results as development results.
9. **The pessimistic total is $14.4 of $15.** If the cap is reached, the run stops and stays resumable, but some claims
   may be left unrun.
